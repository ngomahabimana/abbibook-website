About Me

Joseph Habimana Ngoma is a passionate Christian author, spiritual teacher, and refugee survivor from Bukavu, Democratic Republic of Congo. Today, he resides in the United States, where he continues to write, preach, and inspire others through the message of God’s Kingdom.

He is the author of over 9 books, including Why Me?, I Am Stronger Than Strongman, and the Kingdom Builders series — books written to reveal truth, expose darkness, and empower believers to walk in their divine identity and authority.

Joseph’s mission is rooted in John 8:32 – “You will know the truth, and the truth will set you free.” He believes that every human being was created for a divine purpose, and that spiritual knowledge is the key to transformation and freedom.

Through his writing and teaching, he equips people with faith, understanding, and strength to overcome darkness, rediscover God’s design for humanity, and live as true Kingdom citizens.
